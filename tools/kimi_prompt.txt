I'm building a Binance SPOT pump-and-dump ONSET detector in Python (asyncio). I want your best methodology. No need to write code or touch files — I want a dense technical design critique with concrete numbers.

## Goal
Signal the very BEGINNING of a sharp rise ("pump") and the mirror case (sharp drop, "dump") in a crypto pair. NOT after-the-fact confirmation. Target latency ~10 seconds. The user's own framing of what they want detected: "in the last 10 seconds suddenly a lot of volume is flowing".

## Verified constraints
- Binance spot, PUBLIC market data only (no API keys needed).
- Universe: ~700 USDT-quoted pairs; realistically the 300-500 most liquid ones.
- Combined websocket streams, up to 1024 streams per connection:
  wss://stream.binance.com:9443/stream?streams=btcusdt@miniTicker/ethusdt@miniTicker/...
- Per-symbol stream payloads I have confirmed work:
  * @miniTicker, 1 msg/sec/symbol: {c close, o open24h, h high24h, l low24h, v base vol 24h, q quote vol 24h}
  * @ticker, 1 msg/sec/symbol: same as miniTicker PLUS n = trade count, b/B best bid+qty, a/A best ask+qty, P = pct change
  * @aggTrade, every aggregated trade: {p price, q qty, T trade time ms, m = "buyer is market maker"}. So m=false means the taker was a BUYER (aggressive buy), m=true means taker was a SELLER. Critical detail: m=false is a market BUY.
  * @bookTicker: real-time best bid/ask, very high message rate.
- REST: /api/v3/klines (supports 1s, 1m, ... intervals), /api/v3/ticker/24hr (all symbols, weight 80), /api/v3/ticker/price (all symbols, weight 4). Spot REST budget = 6000 weight/min. klines weight = 2.

## My planned design (critique this hard)
1. One combined @miniTicker connection for the tracked universe (~400 streams). Per symbol, maintain 1-second buckets of (price, cumulative 24h quote volume q). Then quote-volume flow over the last 10s = q_now - q_10s_ago (a delta of the rolling 24h counter). Compare that against a rolling baseline to get a "volume burst multiple" and a z-score.
2. For symbols that pass a cheap volume+price-velocity gate, dynamically SUBSCRIBE on the same connection to that symbol's @aggTrade for a few minutes to measure taker buy/sell imbalance at sub-second resolution and confirm direction.
3. Score, rank, alert.
