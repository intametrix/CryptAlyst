1. What this is for

CryptAlyst watches the whole of Binance — several hundred pairs at once — and answers three questions in that order: is anything moving, what does the model expect next, and was the last expectation right.

It is a market-watch and forecasting bench, not an execution system. Nothing here places an order. Its value is that every claim it makes can be traced to a recorded number, including the occasions when it declined to make one.

3. The overview

Six panels, each answering a different question, all live:

panelwhat it is telling you
live alertsdetections as they happen, newest first — a pump, a dump, a reversal. Each card carries the numbers that fired it
flow leaderboardevery symbol ranked by a chosen measure: burst multiple, 10s move, volume. This is the panel you will use most
flow splitthe same market separated into spot and perp, so you can see where the pressure is
pump / dump screenthe filtered table: only rows that pass your thresholds, with a verdict and a score

The cards explain themselves. Hover anything with a dotted underline for a second and a half and a plain-English definition appears — what the number is, how it is computed, and what a large value implies.

4. The board and the alerts

A row shows the symbol, whether it is spot or a perpetual, a burst bar with its multiple, and the recent move. The multiple is the readable one: 26.4x means the last ten seconds carried twenty-six times the normal flow for that pair.

Filtering and freezing

  • ALL / PERP / SPOT narrows the board; the count in the header follows.
  • Clicking a sortable column heading sorts by it; click again to reverse.
  • Hovering the list freezes the order, so a row cannot move out from under the pointer. Moving away resumes live ordering.
  • Arrow keys walk the list and light exactly one row; Enter opens that pair's chart.

The alert card

Each card is one detection: the symbol, the direction, the score, the window it fired on and the evidence — price, move, flow multiple, z-score, aggressor share. compact at the top of the feed shrinks the cards to one line each when you want many on screen.

If History says "not enough history", press download more before training. A forecast that cannot hold bars back cannot be graded, and an ungraded forecast is a drawing.

What the decision trail shows you

The platform records the time each forecast was computed. If it is older than a minute, the decision is refused as stale and says so. If it is fresh but projects a move smaller than 0.05%, the verdict is none — no trade. Only a fresh forecast with a real edge produces a buy or sell, with a price and a target. Later, replay grades every one of those against the price that actually arrived.

6. Reading the numbers honestly

numberwhat it meanshow to read it
MAPEmean absolute percentage error of the forecastlower is better; compare only on the same frame and horizon
coveragehow often the actual price landed inside the conea 90% cone should be right about 90% of the time; far off means the cone is lying
R²variance explainedbe careful — on price levels it is flattered by the window's own spread, so a useless model can score 0.97
burstrecent flow versus normala multiple; 10x is a real event, 2x is noise
edgeprojected move against the floorbelow 0.05% the platform will not call a trade, and that is deliberate
z-scorehow unusual a move is for that pairabove 4 is rare; on quiet pairs it is common
The one mistake worth avoiding. Do not read a high R² as skill. The platform's own analysis found the old ranking rewarded models that simply followed the window's drift; that is why the comparison table shows MAPE and coverage next to it, and why the backtest section exists.

8. When something looks wrong

Nothing is silently empty. A panel that cannot load shows why, in place, and the rest of the page keeps working. If a panel is blank with no message, that is a bug worth reporting — it should never happen.

The honest state of the trading evidence

If you open the decision trail today you will mostly see refusals, and occasionally a call. That is the true state: across one hundred and sixty-four recorded forecasts the platform issued seven directional calls and none of them were right yet. The machinery is working — it is the models that have no edge. Treat every buy or sell you see as an experiment being measured, not a recommendation.
